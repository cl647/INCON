About the Team

The Intelligent Neuroimaging for Computational Neuroscience (INCON) group develops multi-scale, interpretable computational approaches that integrate multimodal MRI with spatial transcriptomics, genomics, and histology imaging to characterize brain phenotypes across health and disease. The group translates these phenotypes into clinically useful biomarkers supported by rigorous validation, prospective trials, and real-world evaluation.

Dr Chao Li is a clinician-scientist working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and clinical neuroscience. Drawing on dual expertise in clinical care and machine learning, he develops multi-scale, interpretable computational methods—often integrating multimodal neuroimaging with molecular and histology-derived signals—to characterize multi-scale structural and functional phenotypes in healthy and diseased brains, with a focus on robust validation and clinical translation.
