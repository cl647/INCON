About the Team

The Intelligent Neuroimaging for Computational Neuroscience (INCON) group, led by Dr Chao Li, develops multi-scale, interpretable computational neuroimaging to characterize brain phenotypes across health and disease, and to deliver clinically useful biomarkers through rigorous clinical and real-world evaluation.

Dr Chao Li is a clinician-scientist working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and clinical neuroscience. His research develops multi-scale, interpretable computational methods—often leveraging multimodal neuroimaging—to characterize brain phenotypes across health and disease.
